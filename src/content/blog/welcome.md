## A new way to stay connected

We're excited to launch this blog as a way to stay connected with our partners — a place to share
stories from the field, prayer updates, testimonies of God's faithfulness, and vision for what's
ahead.

### What you'll find here

- **Field updates** — what God is doing across Nepal and beyond
- **Testimonies** — real stories of lives touched by the Gospel
- **Prayer requests** — specific needs you can lift up
- **Vision** — where the mission is heading next

We'll post **about twice a month** — enough to stay in the loop, without overwhelming your inbox.

> "For the earth will be filled with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD as the waters cover the sea." — Habakkuk 2:14

Thank you for partnering with us. Stay tuned — the best is yet to come.
